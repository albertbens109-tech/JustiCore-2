Cases

Every report received through the worker channel, the hotline and field partners. Select a case to triage it, refer it or record contact.

Accountability register

When a report points to a workplace failure, Justicore issues a notice to the estate operator or factory management. A report from an anonymous worker is issued as a pattern notice that does not identify the worker. The register tracks acknowledgement, remediation and escalation.
